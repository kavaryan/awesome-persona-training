# Alignment-oriented LLM persona and character training

Map research on training or shaping persistent assistant character, dispositions,
identity and values for AI alignment, and on safety consequences of such changes.
Include mechanistic studies of trait representations and generalization when they
explain or control assistant behavior. Keep enabling foundations clearly separate.
General fictional role-playing, entertainment character fidelity, user
personalization and personality imitation without an assistant-safety contribution
are outside scope. Generic RLHF and generic jailbreak papers are not sufficient.
Read abstracts or full text. A relevant paper need not mention persona or character
in its title; conversely these words do not establish relevance.

Tag intervention type, affected traits, safety consequence, and evidence type.
Distinguish prompting, activation steering, training and conceptual proposals.
