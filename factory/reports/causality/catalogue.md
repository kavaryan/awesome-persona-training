- [Causal Detection of Multi-Step LLM Agent Attacks](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/mothukuri26a.html) (2026-09-29) — Models agent execution graphs to detect injection-dependent harm and reconstruct attack chains using structural counterfactual calculations.
  Scope: Directly targets causal attribution of prompt-injection outcomes in observable agent trajectories. Counterfactuals are calculated in an assumed structural model, not established by executing repaired trajectories.
  application_purpose: attack detection or defense, failure attribution or repair. Performs post-hoc injection detection and forensic attribution of harmful actions to injection sources and attack-chain bottlenecks.
  causal_framework_and_identification: structural or actual-causality assumptions. SCM counterfactuals assume correct additive equations, no hidden confounders, and independent node noise for chain products; coefficients use benchmark attack rates.
  effect_measure: other explicit measure. For observed harm, necessity is scored as 1−P(M=1|do(I=0)); the example multiplies counterfactual chain activations to obtain 0.011 and a necessity score of 0.989.
  interaction_setting: agent-tool or environment, external-information interaction, multi-agent. Analyzes web and tool agents consuming external content, including multi-agent benchmarks; evidence combines synthetic, benchmark, and real-scenario trajectories.
  intervention_granularity: token or span. The modeled intervention removes injection content at an observation node and propagates injection absence through the chain; no executed agent replay is demonstrated.
  temporal_system_representation: message sequence or execution trajectory, provenance or dependency graph, structural or time-unrolled causal model. Complete logged action histories become graphs with data, trust, and state edges; structural equations model node activation and harmful chain completion.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/mothukuri26a/mothukuri26a.pdf); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

  interaction_setting: multi-agent, agent-tool or environment. LLM agents interact in two simulated environments across four architectures and two base models.
  intervention_granularity: message or turn. Applies proposition-targeted perturbations to individual messages at a realized decision point; exact replacement constraints and reset protocol are unspecified.
  temporal_system_representation: message sequence or execution trajectory. Analysis conditions on realized decision points within simulations and measures only the next action; history sufficiency is not specified.
  Evidence: [abstract](https://openalex.org/W7204125254); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

  application_purpose: attack detection or defense, runtime monitoring, failure attribution or repair. Detects cascade onset and ranks origins, relay agents, amplifiers, and propagation paths during execution.
  causal_framework_and_identification: observational discovery or temporal prediction. LI-CTE estimates history-aware directed dependence; sufficient channel visibility is assumed, while confounding controls and intervention identification are not established.
  effect_measure: predictive or information-dependence score. LI-CTE scores directed agent-to-agent dependence across channels and history; exact variables and conditioning sets are omitted, with spectral scores separating attack from benign traces.
  interaction_setting: multi-agent, agent-tool or environment, persistent-memory interaction. Executed AutoGen, CrewAI, MetaGPT, and debate traces span communication, memory, tool, and execution channels under benign and attack scenarios.
  intervention_granularity: no intervention. The attribution estimator observes traces without replay or interventions; benchmark attacks inject adversarial content, but exact manipulation units are incompletely supplied.
  temporal_system_representation: message sequence or execution trajectory, provenance or dependency graph. Maintains a dynamic directed influence tensor across agents, channels, and turns, then monitors its spectral evolution.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19240); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

## Supporting methods and foundations

- [Which Agent Causes Task Failures and When? On Automated Failure Attribution of LLM Multi-Agent Systems](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/zhang25cq.html) (2025-10-06) — Defines decisive errors counterfactually and benchmarks LLM judges that locate responsible agents and steps in failed multi-agent logs.
  Scope: Provides a specific benchmark and judgment baseline for causal failure attribution, but its empirical methods predict human annotations rather than execute or identify the defined counterfactual repairs.
  application_purpose: failure attribution or repair. Locates the agent and earliest decisive error step to guide debugging of failed LLM collaborations.
  causal_framework_and_identification: heuristic or unsupported causal identification. Defines decisive errors by hypothetical repair success and selects the earliest; LLM judges and human labels do not establish those intervention effects.
  effect_measure: actual-cause indicator or minimal-repair size, other explicit measure. Conceptual indicator tests original failure versus success after action-and-suffix correction; experiments measure agent and step prediction accuracy against annotations.
  interaction_setting: multi-agent, agent-tool or environment. Uses failure logs from 127 tool-equipped LLM systems solving GAIA and AssistantBench tasks through turn-based collaboration.
  intervention_granularity: message or turn, multi-step segment or trajectory. Conceptually replaces an erroneous action and corrects its suffix while preserving the prefix; empirical attribution uses log judgments without demonstrated repair execution.
  temporal_system_representation: message sequence or execution trajectory, state-transition system. Defines trajectories of states and actions with one active agent per time step and a binary terminal failure outcome.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v267/main/assets/zhang25cq/zhang25cq.pdf); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

- [Responsibility and Blame: A Structural-Model Approach](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2003/21) (2003) — Defines structural-model responsibility through minimal contingencies and blame through epistemic expectations.
  Scope: Supplies precise foundational measures for attribution under redundant or interacting causes. Its explicit application to state responsibility for temporal specifications connects the method to formal agent execution auditing, while no LLM interaction application is demonstrated.
  application_purpose: credit assignment, failure attribution or repair. Formally allocates causal responsibility and epistemic blame; discusses specification adequacy, redundant states, and system fault tolerance.
  causal_framework_and_identification: structural or actual-causality assumptions. Extends Halpern–Pearl actual causality using actuality, contingency dependence, stability, and minimality in recursive models. Responsibility presumes known equations and context; blame averages across epistemically possible model-context pairs.
  effect_measure: Chockler-Halpern responsibility, blame score. For candidate X=x and outcome proposition phi, responsibility is zero for noncauses and otherwise 1/(k+1), where k is the fewest contingency variables changed to satisfy the actual-cause conditions. Blame weights post-action responsibility by prior epistemic probabilities, excluding situations where the outcome or action already held.
  interaction_setting: abstract interaction model. Uses abstract voting, preemption, and action-outcome examples, plus program-verification applications. No observed conversational agents are analyzed.
  intervention_granularity: joint set of units. Formally sets a candidate variable and contingency variables to alternative values, checking outcome dependence and stability under restoring subsets. Units are abstract variables or state propositions, not conversational spans; no empirical replay occurs.
  temporal_system_representation: structural or time-unrolled causal model, state-transition system. Uses recursive structural equations with exogenous contexts and endogenous variables. Blame distinguishes before-action and after-action variables; verification examples concern states satisfying eventuality specifications.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2003/21); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

- [On the Pitfalls of Measuring Emergent Communication](https://ifaamas.org/Proceedings/aamas2019/pdfs/p693.pdf) (date unverified) — Shows that message–action correlations can persist without communicative influence and evaluates interventions on received messages.
  Scope: Supplies a direct methodological foundation for distinguishing signaling from causal listening in agent conversations, using discrete-message RL agents rather than LLMs.
  application_purpose: behavior or communication interpretation, explanation evaluation. Tests whether communication metrics reveal actual effects on listeners, supporting interpretable monitoring of agent behavior.
  causal_framework_and_identification: controlled counterfactual replay, randomized intervention. Evaluates listener policies under alternative message inputs and tests randomly replaced communication during training; observational signaling metrics are explicitly distinguished from influence.
  effect_measure: other explicit measure, predictive or information-dependence score. One-step CIC assesses listener next-action distribution sensitivity to changed messages; its formula is omitted here, while speaker consistency measures message–action dependence.
  interaction_setting: multi-agent, agent-tool or environment. Deep RL agents exchange discrete symbols through bidirectional channels in simulated matrix games.
  intervention_granularity: message or turn, agent, communication edge, or channel. Changes received symbols for trained-policy evaluation and uniformly scrambles messages during separate training experiments; architecture ablations are additional controls.
  temporal_system_representation: state-transition system, partially observed process, message sequence or execution trajectory. Markov-game framing separates communication and environmental actions; iterated experiments condition policies on the preceding five rounds of actions and messages.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://ifaamas.org/Proceedings/aamas2019/pdfs/p693.pdf); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

- [On Notions of Causality and Distributed Knowledge](https://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~meyden/research/distk-full.pdf) (date unverified) — Develops formal connections between causal information-flow constraints and distributed knowledge in deterministic multi-agent executions.
  Scope: Supplies methodological background for reasoning about permitted influence through interaction histories: trace purging, observation equivalence, and interleaving-sensitive knowledge. It contains formal proofs rather than LLM experiments.
  application_purpose: behavior or communication interpretation. Characterizes which agents’ actions can convey information to another agent under information-flow security policies.
  causal_framework_and_identification: structural or actual-causality assumptions. Formal noninterference trace semantics and epistemic equivalences assume deterministic immediate effects; converse results require acyclic policies, not statistical adjustment.
  effect_measure: other explicit measure. Boolean security conditions require equal agent observations for traces with equal purged histories or permitted-information representations; no numeric causal effect is estimated.
  interaction_setting: abstract interaction model, multi-agent. Abstract agents or security domains interact through deterministic actions and observations governed by directed noninterference policies.
  intervention_granularity: multi-step segment or trajectory, joint set of units. Formal purge operators delete selected agents’ actions from traces; proofs compare alternative traces and action interleavings, without empirical replay.
  temporal_system_representation: state-transition system, message sequence or execution trajectory. Deterministic transitions generate action traces and local views with asynchronous perfect recall; stuttering observations are collapsed and interleavings matter.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~meyden/research/distk-full.pdf); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

- [Counterfactual Influence in Markov Decision Processes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08514) (date unverified) — Influence-constrained MDP counterfactuals preserve dependence on the observed trajectory while seeking improved action sequences.
  Scope: Offers a specific diagnostic for agent-trace replay: alternative trajectories can cease to reflect the observed episode under a chosen causal model. This supports evaluating counterfactual explanations, although applications are grid-world and healthcare MDPs.
  application_purpose: explanation evaluation, failure attribution or repair. Evaluates whether improved counterfactual action sequences remain informative about the particular observed execution.
  causal_framework_and_identification: structural or actual-causality assumptions, controlled counterfactual replay. Uses known MDP transitions and a Gumbel-Max SCM with exogenous noise conditioned on the observed path; results depend on this structural encoding.
  effect_measure: other explicit measure, outcome probability, rate, or mean change. Influence compares observation-conditioned versus prior next-state probabilities under alternative actions; experiments compare cumulative rewards across influence and action-change bounds.
  interaction_setting: agent-tool or environment, abstract interaction model. Simulated policies act in grid-world, epidemic, and sepsis MDPs; no LLM conversations are studied.
  intervention_granularity: multi-step segment or trajectory, joint set of units. Changes up to m actions from an observed path, holds its initial state fixed, and prunes counterfactual transitions violating influence constraints.
  temporal_system_representation: state-transition system, message sequence or execution trajectory, structural or time-unrolled causal model. Observed state-action sequences condition time-indexed Gumbel-Max counterfactual transitions, with influence constraints spanning up to k steps.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.08514); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

- [CausalLoss-Fin: Attributing Financial-Agent Loss to Decisions and Infrastructure Faults](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25960) (date unverified) — Uses exact replay to separate financial losses caused by agent decisions from losses caused by infrastructure messages, including joint repairs.
  Scope: Explicitly extends agent-step attribution methods with intervenable environment faults and minimal repairs. Its deterministic programmatic policies provide methodological evidence, while applicability to stochastic LLM agents remains untested.
  application_purpose: failure attribution or repair, credit assignment, explanation evaluation. Separates decision and infrastructure contributions to monetary loss and tests whether repairing attributed faults restores outcomes.
  causal_framework_and_identification: controlled counterfactual replay, structural or actual-causality assumptions, game-theoretic attribution. Deterministic structural equations and fixed exogenous inputs support exact replay; minimal sufficient repairs handle joint faults, while Shapley allocates the infrastructure contrast.
  effect_measure: outcome probability, rate, or mean change, actual-cause indicator or minimal-repair size, game-theoretic attribution score. Infrastructure effect is L(π,w)−L(π,w0); step regret is loss avoided by the best listed alternative; minimal repair size and signed message Shapley allocations are also reported.
  interaction_setting: agent-tool or environment. Programmatic policies use eleven tools across payment processor, ledger, ERP, and bank-feed simulations with independently faulted delivery streams.
  intervention_granularity: message or turn, tool call or result, joint set of units. Repairs named delivery faults or forces a step action, then reruns the same policy with case and other schedule inputs fixed; joint repair search is capped at three messages.
  temporal_system_representation: structural or time-unrolled causal model, message sequence or execution trajectory, partially observed process. An SCM links hidden case facts, message schedules, time-indexed system views, action-observation histories, executed effects, and terminal monetary loss.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.25960); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

- [Causal Past Logic for Runtime Verification of Distributed LLM Agent Workflows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20923) (date unverified) — Causal Past Logic monitors locally visible event histories in asynchronous LLM workflows and proves guard evaluation correct.
  Scope: Provides a specific formal-trace foundation for defining which messages and results are available at an agent decision, relevant to valid replay contexts and monitors. Its causality is message-order visibility, not identified intervention effects.
  application_purpose: runtime monitoring. Guards branch and loop decisions using visible test and security histories, including checks that results match the current patch candidate.
  causal_framework_and_identification: not-reported. Uses formal happens-before semantics and proves monitor correctness; no statistical or counterfactual causal-identification framework is supplied.
  effect_measure: not-reported. Reports monitor latency, metadata size, and guard correctness, without a causal-effect estimand.
  interaction_setting: multi-agent, agent-tool or environment. Asynchronous agent lifelines exchange reports; a code-review example connects an orchestrator, test runner, security reviewer, and committer.
  intervention_granularity: no intervention. Temporal guards enact workflow choices; the study does not estimate effects of conversation or execution interventions.
  temporal_system_representation: message sequence or execution trajectory, provenance or dependency graph. Valued message sequence charts combine local event order with send-receive edges; vector clocks track each lifeline's latest visible events.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20923); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

- [Causal Attribution for Agentic Decisions: Estimators, Coupling, and a Traceability Specification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06445) (date unverified) — Develops trajectory-level total and direct effect estimands, analyzes replay coupling failures, and derives logging requirements for agent attribution.
  Scope: Makes a direct methodological contribution to causal analysis of agent execution steps, supported by analytic constructions and synthetic estimator checks. It explicitly leaves the live deployment comparison unrun, so it supplies methodological evidence rather than demonstrated auditing of deployed agents.
  application_purpose: behavior or communication interpretation, explanation evaluation. Determines what step attribution measures and what records would permit explanation audits. Comparison of deployed observability rankings with causal rankings is preregistered, not performed.
  causal_framework_and_identification: structural or actual-causality assumptions, controlled counterfactual replay, mediation identification. Contrasts fresh downstream noise with factual-noise replay and a pinned-downstream direct-effect arm. Identification requires structural equations, valid interventions, recorded environment state, and appropriate sampling coupling. Synthetic validation does not establish replay fidelity in deployed systems; seed control alone is explicitly insufficient.
  effect_measure: outcome probability, rate, or mean change, mediation effect, other distributional distance or divergence. Marginal total effect is expected terminal Y after step resampling and fresh downstream noise minus factual Y; CRN effect fixes factual downstream noise. Direct effect pins downstream actions, and mediated effect is the residual. Synthetic Y equals w*a1+(1-w)*a3. Coupling analysis compares categorical distributions p and logit-perturbed q using total variation and sampling agreement; no KL effect is reported.
  interaction_setting: abstract interaction model. Models agent pipelines containing model calls, retrievals, tools, memory operations, and branches. Demonstrated evidence uses a planted chain and synthetic sampling distributions, not a live regulated pipeline.
  intervention_granularity: tool call or result, message or turn, not-reported. Primary operator resamples a step action under the unchanged policy; other adopted operators force actions or observations, change context, or change policy. Downstream actions either regenerate or are pinned. Concrete text-span granularity is not supplied.
  temporal_system_representation: message sequence or execution trajectory, structural or time-unrolled causal model. Steps draw actions conditional on the prefix and exogenous noise; a terminal outcome depends on step actions. A four-step structural chain separates inert, retrieval, and executing components.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.06445); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

- [Agent Incentives: A Causal Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.01685) (date unverified) — Develops structural causal criteria for agents’ information, control, response, and instrumental incentives.
  Scope: Provides methodological background for auditing whether observations change agent decisions or agents benefit from influencing users and overseers, with explicit safety examples but no LLM conversation evaluation.
  application_purpose: alignment auditing, behavior or communication interpretation. Analyzes incentives for unfair decisions, user manipulation, and reward tampering through formal safety examples.
  causal_framework_and_identification: structural or actual-causality assumptions. Assumes known acyclic causal structure, independent exogenous errors, and optimal policies; nested counterfactuals support sound and complete graphical incentive criteria.
  effect_measure: action or trajectory change, outcome probability, rate, or mean change. Response incentives compare D_x with D under shared exogenous conditions; control criteria compare expected utility with altered control or nested interventions through X.
  interaction_setting: abstract interaction model, human-agent. Abstract decision agents interact with environments; a hypothetical recommender influences user opinions and clicks.
  intervention_granularity: not-reported. Formal interventions replace structural variables or functions and alter information availability; no message, tool, or trace-level unit is specified.
  temporal_system_representation: structural or time-unrolled causal model. Single-decision causal influence diagrams distinguish observations, decisions, utilities, and structural variables; repeated interactions are abstracted rather than traced.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://arxiv.org/html/2102.01685); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

- [Actual Causality and Responsibility Attribution in Decentralized Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.00302) (date unverified) — Defines actual causes and agent responsibility in partially observed multi-agent sequential decisions, with simulation experiments.
  Scope: Provides methodological foundations for trace attribution: interventions must respect agents' information states, causal dependencies, and minimal contingencies. Evaluation concerns simulated game agents rather than LLM conversations.
  application_purpose: credit assignment, failure attribution or repair. Attributes responsibility for realized outcomes to agents and their actions to support accountable sequential decision making.
  causal_framework_and_identification: structural or actual-causality assumptions. Extends but-for and HP causality with information-state eligibility and cause-witness minimality; experiments assume known policies and all exogenous context variables.
  effect_measure: Chockler-Halpern responsibility, actual-cause indicator or minimal-repair size, other explicit measure. For outcome φ, CH maximizes m/(|A|+|W|), with m the agent's actions in cause A and W its contingency; extensions weight cause-witness ratios and exclude own contingency actions from denominators.
  interaction_setting: multi-agent, abstract interaction model, agent-tool or environment. Models interacting agents as Dec-POMDPs and evaluates attribution in simulated Goofspiel games.
  intervention_granularity: joint set of units. Replaces individual or joint action variables under fixed SCM context; actual-cause actions must retain their original information states in the counterfactual.
  temporal_system_representation: structural or time-unrolled causal model, partially observed process, state-transition system. The SCM indexes states, observations, information states, and actions by agent and time; policies depend on information states derived from interaction history.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://arxiv.org/html/2204.00302); review: bootstrap-code-agent.

- [A Language-Based Causal Model for Safety](https://hcantunc.github.io/papers/tase22/paper.pdf) (date unverified) — Defines automata-based actual causes and safe alternative executions for regular-language hazards.
  Scope: Provides specific formal background for agent trace repair: message-passing automata encode communication events, minimal hazardous traces, and inserted events that avert hazards. No LLM application is demonstrated.
  application_purpose: failure attribution or repair. Explains hazardous executions and computes safe alternatives for debugging formal systems.
  causal_framework_and_identification: structural or actual-causality assumptions. Adapts Halpern–Pearl-style sufficiency, counterfactual contingencies, and minimality to automata; causes may enable hazards while decorated traces must avoid them.
  effect_measure: actual-cause indicator or minimal-repair size. Tests whether a trace minimally enables a regular-language hazard and whether its decorated alternatives avoid that hazard; no probabilistic effect is estimated.
  interaction_setting: abstract interaction model. Finite automata communicate through synchronized input/output actions; evaluation uses generated automata and a railway example.
  intervention_granularity: multi-step segment or trajectory. Constructs model-valid alternative traces by interleaving additional actions with a candidate causal trace to prevent hazard occurrence and enablement.
  temporal_system_representation: state-transition system, message sequence or execution trajectory. Finite automata represent execution words and regular-language hazards, with parallel composition for message-passing systems.
  Evidence: [full-text](https://hcantunc.github.io/papers/tase22/paper.pdf); review: bootstrap-code-agent.
